01 — CUSTOMER-FACING AI

Mira

Multi-tenant AI business representative

01

An AI representative that answers product questions with business-specific catalogue context.

  • Next.js App Router
  • TypeScript
  • Supabase
  • Gemini + Groq

Problem

Give each business a customer-facing AI representative that can respond using its own product and order context.

Constraints

Multi-tenant business data; answers need catalogue detail and stock awareness, multilingual support, and tolerance for imperfect product queries.

Approach

Built with Next.js App Router and TypeScript, using Supabase Postgres and Auth. Gemini handles AI responses with Groq as fallback. Capabilities include catalogue admin CRUD, stock-aware answers, multilingual support, fuzzy search, manual order tracking, and Paystack payments.

Documented capability

A live product with business-specific AI conversations and the named catalogue, search, order-tracking, and payment capabilities. No measured business outcomes are claimed.
