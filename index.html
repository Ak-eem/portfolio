01

Mira

Multi-tenant AI business representative

An AI representative that answers product questions with business-specific catalogue context.

  • Next.js App Router
  • TypeScript
  • Supabase Postgres / Auth
  • Gemini with Groq fallback

Problem

Give each business a customer-facing AI representative that can respond using its own product and order context.

Constraints

Multi-tenant business data; answers need to account for catalogue details and stock, support multiple languages, and handle imperfect product queries.

Approach

Built with Next.js App Router and TypeScript, using Supabase Postgres and Auth. Gemini handles AI responses with Groq as a fallback. Product catalogue and admin CRUD, stock-aware answers, multilingual support, fuzzy search, manual order tracking, and Paystack are included capabilities.

Documented capability

A live product with business-specific AI conversations, catalogue administration, stock-aware answers, multilingual support, fuzzy search, manual order tracking, and Paystack payment support. No measured business outcomes are claimed.
