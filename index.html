01 / AI productProduct engineering

Mira

A multi-tenant AI online business representative—built to give each business a useful, conversational presence.

Live site ↗ Repository ↗
Problem
Businesses need an online representative that can respond in context, rather than a one-size-fits-all chat experience.
System
A multi-tenant product built with Next.js 16 App Router, React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, and Supabase Postgres and Auth.
Engineering decision
Gemini 3.6 Flash handles the primary model path, with Groq running Llama 3.3 70B as a fallback. Deployed on Vercel; product domain: miraapp.com.ng.

03 / Applied MLResearch build

Sentinel

A crypto trading bot exploring predictive signals and disciplined risk handling.

Private build · no public repository

Problem
Trading signals need to be considered alongside a defined approach to exits and risk.
System
A crypto bot using a HistGradientBoostingClassifier for its predictive signal.
Engineering decision
Pair the classifier with a triple-barrier risk method to make trade outcome boundaries part of the system design.
