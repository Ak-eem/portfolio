Mira

Commerce · AI

Constraint

Keep product discovery and customer answers grounded in a merchant’s catalogue and stock.

On record

The case study describes stock-aware answers, catalogue CRUD, multilingual support, fuzzy search and manual order tracking.

Boundary to understand

The chat path uses a service role that bypasses database RLS, so it scopes requests by business_id; admin access uses session-bound Supabase RLS.
